OUR LIST-BUILDING STRATEGY

THREE WAYS WE CONNECT WITH OUR CUSTOMERS

Our email marketing strategy focuses on building relationships with customers who are genuinely interested in skateboarding and streetwear.

Rather than collecting email addresses without permission, SIDETRACKED will encourage customers to voluntarily subscribe by providing immediate benefits and long-term value.

01

IN-STORE CUSTOMERS

WHERE & HOW WE COLLECT EMAILS

Customers visiting Sidetrack Skate Shop will be invited to join our email list during checkout.

A QR code displayed near the register will direct customers to our online sign-up form.

IMMEDIATE VALUE

Customers receive a 15% discount code after confirming their email address.

LONG-TERM VALUE

Subscribers receive exclusive promotions, new product announcements, early access to merchandise, and local skateboarding event updates.

02

ONLINE SHOPPERS

WHERE & HOW WE COLLECT EMAILS

Visitors browsing the SIDETRACKED website will see an email sign-up form.

We will also promote the email club through Instagram, TikTok, and other organic social media posts.

IMMEDIATE VALUE

New subscribers receive a 15% welcome discount after completing email confirmation.

LONG-TERM VALUE

Subscribers receive early access to new product drops, exclusive online promotions, and updates about upcoming collections.

03

LOCAL SKATE COMMUNITY

WHERE & HOW WE COLLECT EMAILS

SIDETRACKED will promote its email list at local skateboarding events, skateparks, and community gatherings.

Event flyers and QR codes will encourage participants to voluntarily subscribe.

IMMEDIATE VALUE

Subscribers receive a digital guide to local skate spots and access to exclusive event promotions after confirming their email.

LONG-TERM VALUE

Members receive invitations to community events, skate sessions, product releases, and special collaborations with local skaters.
